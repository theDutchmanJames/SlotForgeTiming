SlotForge Track Builder 0.2.2 integration runtime, provided by the project owner
in SlotForge-Timing-integration-v0.2.2-handoff.zip (29 October 2026).

integration.js, core.js, geometry.js and geometry.cjs are the supplied runtime.
The only local vendor change is core.js's CommonJS geometry path, adjusted to
./geometry.cjs. Browser load order: geometry.js, core.js, integration.js.
No npm runtime dependencies. Geometry is derived from bounded templates; files
are data, not scripts. Contracts 1.0.0-draft.1 and 1.0.0-draft.2 are supported.
