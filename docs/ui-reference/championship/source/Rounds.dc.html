Every session uses one of your race setups, so a championship can mix a short sprint with a long final. The kind (Practice, Qualifying or Race) comes from the race setup. The track of the round is chosen for you when you load the session on the Home tab. A round that is driven keeps its results: changing its sessions afterwards does not change points that were already awarded.
What the championship needs from the race setups
Race: a race, a timed race or a heat race. Points come from the final result. In an Endurance Race the heats count as one race.
Qualifying: sets the order for the next race of the round (as today) and gives the pole bonus, if you set one.
Practice: never gives points.
