Points, drops and roster
Points for the finishing places Races only
Start from a ready-made table (you can change every number)
A car that was disqualified or did not finish gets no points for the race. A car that finished, even many laps down, counts as a finisher.
Bonus points
Bonus points are added to the points of the race of the same round. The fastest lap is the best valid lap of the race session; a lap with a penalty that took it away does not count.
Dropped results
Best 5 of 6 rounds count. A dropped result is shown crossed out in the standings. Drivers and teams are dropped on their own: a team can drop a round that none of its drivers dropped.
