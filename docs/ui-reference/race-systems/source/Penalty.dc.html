In plain words

Every 5 penalty points add 5 seconds to the race time, so 12 points are 10 seconds. The Race Director can also give a Drivethrough or a Stop-and-Go (3 special penalties). A driver is disqualified at 20 points, and every lap driven on an empty tank gives 2 points.

BasicsHow do penalty points turn into time?
Points turn into time
Every points add seconds
Choose Steps and a field appears for them: points=seconds, separated by commas (5=5, 10=12, 15=30).
12 points are 10 seconds. Only full blocks of 5 points count, so 4 points are no time at all and 5 points are 5 seconds.
Special penaltiesWhat can the Race Director give besides points?
Penalties the Race Director can give instead of leaving points to turn into time. One costs points, and must be served by stopping on the sensor in the pits. A Stop-and-Go needs the car to stand still for its seconds; a Drivethrough is served when the pit stop starts.
