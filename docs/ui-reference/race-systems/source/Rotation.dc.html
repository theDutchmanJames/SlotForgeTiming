In plain words

8 drivers race in 6 heats on 4 lanes, with up to 4 cars in a heat. Everybody races 3 heats. The lanes are not quite even: P7 races lane 3 twice and P8 races lane 4 twice. Heat 1 is locked, so a shuffle leaves it where it is. Juniors (P5 to P8) can be put in a heat in one go.

The small parts

The menus, and what the page says when something is not right. Same colours, same dots: green is filled in, open ring is nothing changed, amber needs a look.

Tools (the button above the heats)
Shuffle the heat orderPuts the heats in another order. Locked heats stay where they are.
Reverse the orderTurns the order of the heats round. Locked heats stay where they are.
Shuffle the lanesPuts the participants of every heat in other lanes.
Even out the lanesGives out the lanes again so that everybody drives in every lane about equally often. Who races in which heat stays as it is.
The three dots in a heat row
Copy this heatCopies it right below itself.
Empty this heatTakes everybody out of this heat.
