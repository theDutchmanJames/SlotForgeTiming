In plain words

A car at its best lap is flat out, 3% over it is normal pace and 8% over it is the most saving pace. Flat out wears the tyres 40% more and uses 25% more fuel. The most saving pace wears them 30% less and uses 20% less. The effect starts at lap 3, and a lap under the yellow flag or the Safety Car counts as saving.
