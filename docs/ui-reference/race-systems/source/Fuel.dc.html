In plain words

The tank holds 120 litres and a car uses 2 litres a lap at the target lap time of 9.5 seconds, so a full tank lasts 60 laps. When the fuel runs out, the car retires from the race. Refuelling in the pits runs at 10 litres a second. A fast lap can use up to 25% more fuel and a slow lap can save up to 30%.
