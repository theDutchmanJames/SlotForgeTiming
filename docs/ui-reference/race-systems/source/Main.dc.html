Race systems

Seven systems, one window

Every system page gets the window of Race Setups: the name and the main facts at the top, a side rail with a row for every section, one sentence in plain words, and sections that fold. Nothing about what a system does changes. The same settings are kept in the same way, so every system you have opens as it was.

The frame, the same on every page

ColourEach system keeps the colour it has today: the one on its tab in Race Setup and on its folder on the race screen. It takes the place of the red of Race Setups: the bar at the top, the picture, the small capitals, the open section, the chosen cards, the switches and the Save button. The picture is the one of its tab too.
HeaderThe picture of the system, its kind in small capitals, the name you type (the dashed line says you can), and pills with the main facts. Green Ready to use when it has a name, amber Give it a name when it has not.
Side railA row for every section: its picture, its name, a short note of what it holds now, and a dot. Sections you rarely change sit under Only if you need it.
Used byAt the bottom of the rail: the race setups that use this system. When there are none, it says where to choose it.
In plain wordsOne sentence that reads the whole system back to you, with the numbers in bold. It changes while you type.
SectionsOpen or closed. A closed section shows one line of what is inside. The tables you add rows to (levels, incidents, failures, special penalties, tyres) keep every column they have now.
FooterCancel, and Save with the name of the system, so you always know what you are saving.

The dot on a rail row

Green: filled in, or changed from the standard.
Amber: something that is needed is missing, such as the name.
Open ring: nothing changed from the standard.

What I chose without asking

The name is typed in the header, as in Race Setups. Sections follow the groups the forms have now. Two places are joined: in Tyre wear, Where it applies (practice and qualifying) goes into Basics; in the Safety car system, Lap times and Pit stops become one section, because pit stops holds a single number. Used by is a list for now, not links. Penalty and Damage stay two separate systems.
