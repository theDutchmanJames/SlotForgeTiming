In plain words

Wear is counted per lap, in races only. A new Hard tyre lasts about 50 laps, a Soft about 22 and a Supersoft about 14. Worn tyres hinder the car through the fuel use and the refuelling speed, and at 0% a car gets 2 penalty points every lap. Changing tyres takes 4 seconds, at the same time as the other work in the stop. The widgets turn amber at 25% life left and red at 10%.

Soft. New tyres give the fresh bonus (+5%), and the bonus fades while the tyre wears. At 30% life left it is gone. Below that the tyre starts to hinder the car, and at 0% the hindrance is at its worst (−30%). A plus helps (less fuel used, faster refuelling), a minus hinders. Lap times are never changed.
