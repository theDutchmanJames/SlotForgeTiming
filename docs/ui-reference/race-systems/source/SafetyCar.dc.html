In plain words

The Safety Car is out while the yellow flag is up. Cars must drive laps slower than 15 seconds, and a lap that is too fast gives 1 penalty point. The work in a pit stop takes 50% less time. Random yellow flags are on: every race gets at least 2 flags, somewhere between 10% and 90% of the race and at least 30 seconds apart. Each flag lasts 30 seconds and ends by itself.
