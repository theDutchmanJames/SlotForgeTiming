In plain words

Thursday Cup 2026 has 5 rounds with 16 sessions, and 8 drivers in the roster. 6 sessions are driven; round 3 is next. Only a race gives points: 25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2 and 1 for the first ten, and 1 more for the fastest lap of a driver who finishes in the first 10. The best 4 of 5 rounds count. A sprint gives half the points, rounded to the nearest. A tie goes to the driver with the most wins.

RoundsThe rounds, and the sessions you drive in each
Every session uses one of your race setups, so a championship can mix a short sprint with a long final. The kind comes from the race setup: Race, Qualifying or Practice. A round with no track of its own uses the track of the race setup. A round that is driven keeps its results: changing its sessions afterwards does not change races that were saved. A Time Token Session can not be part of a championship.
A new round starts with the sessions of the round before it.
