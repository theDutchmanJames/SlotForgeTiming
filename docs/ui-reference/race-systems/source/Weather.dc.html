In plain words

The weather is counted in laps of the leader. The rain starts after 8 laps and lasts 6 laps, with 2 laps of cloud before it and 2 laps of clearing after it. Every heat has the same weather. A car has 2 laps to change tyres when the weather asks for it, and gets 2 penalty points, once, for staying on the wrong ones. The wrong tyres also make it use more fuel and refuel slower.
