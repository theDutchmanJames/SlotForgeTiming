Balance of Power
To use itTurn Balance of Power on in a race setup (Race Setups > Balance of Power). It needs a Fuel System, because it works through the fuel rules.
In plain words

Suggest: the app watches the lap times and proposes, and the Race Director accepts. It hinders the fastest car and helps the slowest car, only those two, by 20%. A hindered car uses 20% more fuel per lap and refuels 20% slower; a helped car gets the opposite. The pace of a car is its median lap over the last 5 laps (at least 3). It comes on at a difference of 0.3 s per lap and goes off below 0.15 s, and it is corrected for the length of the lanes.

What changes on the carWhich of the fuel rules are changed?
The fuel a lap uses
Hindered: more fuel per lap. Helped: less. The car has to pit sooner, or drive slower.
How fast it refuels in the pits
Hindered: slower stops. Helped: quicker stops.
The size of the tank
Hindered: a smaller tank. Helped: a bigger one. Fuel in the tank that no longer fits is lost.
It only changes the fuel use, the refuelling and the tank of a car, so it fine-tunes: a car that is several seconds a lap faster will not be caught by it alone. Where a long race or a short tank makes the cars pit, it bites the most. It needs a Fuel System.
