In plain words

A deslot gives a car 1 damage point. From 3 points the car needs a Light damage repair: it stands still on the sensor in the pits for 10 seconds. At 10 points, Critical damage retires the car. The repair and the other work in the stop happen at the same time, so the longest job counts. Car failures are off.

LevelsHow much damage asks for which stop in the pits?
The highest level a car has reached counts. A car must stand still on the sensor in the pits for the seconds of its level: that repairs the damage (the points go back to 0), or retires or disqualifies the car. Leave a gap between the levels, so a hit does not skip one.
