Only races that had a Tyre Wear System get this part. The other results look exactly as they do now.
What is not touched
Lap times, best laps, records and the lap chart are the measured values, never changed by the wear. The wear only shows here as the stints. In a Distance Race the stints are counted in metres (feet) instead of laps: "S · 120 m", with the life left at the end of every stint. The Slotcar statistics page does not get tyre information.
