Rules, pit stops and sets
In a race setup, folder Rule sets (where a race picks its Tyre Wear System)
Like the Damage System and the Weather System: a race picks one. None keeps every race the way it is today.
Where it applies
Never in a Time Token Session. Every race and every heat starts on fresh tyres: the tyre of the slotcar (Database), or the one the Race Director picks before the start in the Tyre Change folder.
What worn tyres do (use either one, or both)
The grip curve of each tyre (previous board) decides how much. Needs Fuel on in the race. It adds up with Balance of Power and the weather, together at most 90%. Lap times and records are never changed.
Works without Fuel too, with the penalty system of the race. Choose Nothing to simulate and show the wear without any penalty.
Without Fuel in the race the wear is still counted and shown, and the 0% rule still works; only the fuel levers are left out.

Together with other pit work (refuelling, repair, driver swap)
A car that leaves before the change is done keeps its worn tyres, like a repair that is not finished. The stop is the car standing on the pit sensor (or the Pit button), with or without Fuel. Rain: when the Weather System asks for Intermediate or Wet tyres they are fitted at the next stop, from the Intermediate and Wet sets, and they go before a tyre that the Race Director had queued.

Distance Racing
Counted per distance, everything above that is counted in laps is counted in metres (feet) instead: the wear is per 100 m, the widgets say how many metres of tyre are left, and the audio says them. A car on a longer lane then wears its tyres faster per lap, as it should. A Distance Race and a System counted per lap can be mixed too: the laps are simply turned into metres with the lane length.
Example: Soft at 30% per 100 m on a lane of 15.4 m wears 4.6% a lap, so a set lasts about 22 laps (330 m).
