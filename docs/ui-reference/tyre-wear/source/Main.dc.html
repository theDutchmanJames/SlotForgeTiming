Sprint wears the tyres quickly (short races, many stops). Endurance wears them slowly. Standard is in between.
Wear is counted per
Distance uses the lane length of the track, so a longer lane wears the tyres more per lap. Choose it for Distance Racing: the numbers below are then per 100 m (per 100 ft), and the widgets say how many metres (feet) of tyre are left.

What the numbers do: the grip curve of one tyre (Soft)
New tyres give the fresh bonus (+5%). The bonus fades while the tyre wears.
At 30% life left the bonus is gone. Below that the tyre starts to hinder the car.
At 0% the hindrance is at its worst (-30%).
A plus helps (less fuel used, faster refuelling), a minus hinders. What exactly changes, and what happens at 0%, is set on the next board. Lap times are never changed.
