# Changelog

Every version of SlotForge Timing, newest first. The README has a section **What to try (x.y.z)** for each version, with what changed, how to try it and what it does not do (yet).

## Versions

- **1.23.0**: damage in the results of heat-based races. The Damage System already worked in every heat; now the results say what it did. The row of every car in a heat has the damage note (*5 damage points, 1 repair (10 seconds in the pits)*, *retired by damage*) and its figures (`damageNote`, `damagePoints`, `repairs`, `repairMs`, `damageOut`), and the standings add it up per driver over the heats and the final (*15 damage points in 2 heats, 1 repair (10 seconds in the pits), retired by damage in 1 heat*). It shows in Results and Records (behind the name, and in the heat lines) and in the PDF. New: `Damage.totalNote`; `H.record` and `H.overall` in `renderer/engine.js`, the standings in `renderer/race.js`. Tests, help, README and CHANGELOG updated.
- **1.22.0**: a speed unit for the tables. New choice **Speed unit in the tables** in App Settings > General > Display: km/h (the standard) or mph. The average speed and the average scale speed in Results and Records (sessions, stints, track records and personal bests) and in the PDF of a session are written in the unit you choose. Distances stay in metres and kilometres; the Speed widget in a layout keeps its own Unit choice. New in `renderer/speed.js`: `UNITS`, `unitOf`, `unitName`; `Stats.text`, `fmtSpeed` and `fmtScale` take the unit. Tests, help, README and CHANGELOG updated.
- **1.21.0**: the app icon is now 1024 x 1024 pixels (it was 512). It is redrawn, not enlarged: the artwork (the dark car on the light grey circle with the red lanes) was traced as a vector drawing, `build/icon.svg`, and `build/icon.png` is that drawing at 1024 px with see-through corners, so the edges are sharp instead of blurry on a Retina Mac (and in a large Windows or Linux icon). The soft grey glow in the windshield and the thin lines of the grille are drawn in too. `build/icon.ico` (Windows) is unchanged. Tests and README updated.
- **1.20.0**: Adjust pad values per race setup. A race setup can have its own values for the five Adjust pads (penalty points, damage points, time in seconds, laps, championship points): a new folder **Adjust pads** in the race setup form (under Yellow flag), one field per pad with up to six values, for example 1, 3, 10. A pad whose field is empty keeps the values from App Settings > Race Director. The values of the setup are used on the Adjust folder of the Race tab, on the Race Director page (phones and tablets get them with the race) and in the race review. The layout (horizontal or vertical) stays a choice of each device. New in `renderer/adjustpad.js`: `ownOf`, `hasOwn` and `forSetup`; the form field type `padlist` tidies the list on save. Tests, help, README and CHANGELOG updated.
- **1.19.0**: order a leaderboard by any value. A new setting **Order by** for every board of the Live Race > Leaderboard grid (under Customize) and for the Leaderboard widget in the Layout Editor: the race position (the standard, as before), the best, last, average or median lap, the personal best on the track, laps, speed, championship points, penalty points, penalty time, damage points, pit stops, fuel left, lane or driver name. **Best or worst on top** says which end comes first (Best first: the fastest lap, the most laps or points, the fewest penalty points...). Cars without a value yet stay at the bottom, equal values keep the race order, the Position column counts the rows of the board, the first row gets the leader look, and From place and To place count in that order. Works in the classic table and in your own row designs, also on phones and tablets. New `renderer/boardorder.js` (tested) and `Timing.ordered` / `Timing.orderValue`.
- **1.18.1**: the arrow and the number of the Delta Timer badge now always fit inside the badge. In the stacked widget (Top and bottom) the text could stick out of the coloured block when the widget was narrow or the difference was long (+1:02.345); now the text shrinks just enough to stay inside, in the Layout Editor (also while you drag the widget to another size), on the Race tab and on the Live Race pages. A badge that already fits looks exactly as before, and the text gets its full size back when the widget gets bigger. New `fit` in `renderer/delta.js`, called from `canvas.js`, `editor.js` and `liveview.js`.
- **1.18.0**: Delta Timers can be stacked. A new setting **Value and badge** in the Layout Editor, in all eight Delta Timers: *Left and right* (the standard, as before) or *Top and bottom*: the time on top and the coloured badge below it, as wide as the widget. The Size of the badge setting then makes the badge narrower instead of shorter. "Left value shows" is now called "Left (or top) value shows". A widget from before is left and right, as it was.
- **1.17.0**: a new Home tab. A welcome band with the date, the track and lights for the Trackmate, sharing on the network and the audio set of the setup. The lanes have a colour each (the colour the layout gives the lane on the race screen, else one of eight), and start lights under them that light up as lanes get ready; a bigger Start button. On the right three cards made from your saved races: the **record to beat** on the track of the setup (with who on the grid is closest), the **last race** with its podium and fastest lap, and **At a glance** (races, laps, distance, most wins). Before the first saved race the right side says what will show up there. New `renderer/homestats.js` (tested without a screen).
- **1.16.2**: "How do I get more voices?" next to the voice in the audio set editor is now a button: a click shows the answer on the page (Windows Settings > Time & language > Speech > Manage voices > Add voices, then restart the app; the Windows 10 way too) and a second click hides it. Before, the answer was only a tooltip.
- **1.16.1**: three things from your notes. The Weather Forecast widget (and the Tyres widget) now shrinks its picture and text to fit when the widget is small, so a longer word such as Cloudy or Clearing no longer pushes the picture out. The Track actions folder says why the Lap, Pit and Call buttons are dimmed (they work while the race runs) and the Lap button says what it does. A **Voice report** button in the audio set editor shows the voices the app is offered and the voice lists of Windows (the Speech_OneCore and classic SAPI registry lists and the Windows Runtime voices), to find out why a voice such as Andrew HD is missing; the voice help says the Natural voices of Narrator are, as far as we know, not offered to other apps. New `voicereport.js`.
- **1.16.0**: dragging in the Rotation Editor (with the mouse, a finger or a pen): a participant onto a place, a place onto another place to swap them, a place back onto the participants to empty it, and a heat by its grip (the dots in the first column) onto another heat to move it; a ghost follows the pointer, the place under it lights up (dashed red when it cannot be done, e.g. a locked heat), Esc lets go, and the page scrolls near the top and the bottom of the window. A colour for every participant (by number, nothing is saved) on the chip, its places and its name, and a picked participant (or a picked place) marks all places of that participant and dims the rest. Clicking works as before; Groups are unchanged. New `renderer/rotationdrag.js`.
- **1.15.0**: the Race tab in coloured folders like the Race Director page (Track actions green, Adjust, Penalties yellow, Balance of Power white, Damage red, Weather blue, Endurance brown; Time tokens teal in a Time Token Session), a Penalties folder with the table and log of the Race Director page instead of the Penalty buttons, no Adjust button any more, and Track actions as a card per lane with large green Lap, orange Pit and amber Call buttons that light up while a car is in the pit or a call waits. New `renderer/dirfolder.js` and `renderer/penaltyui.js` are shared with the Race Director page.
- **1.14.0**: a clearer Rotation Editor: the page in order (name and buttons, size, plans, participants, heats, remarks), real places with an "empty" look, the four plans as buttons with a line each, the number of heats to start from follows the size, a Lock button and a ⋯ menu (copy, empty, delete) on every heat, the shuffles in a Tools menu, a warning with a button that adds a heat for participants who race none, and naming a participant where you pick it. Saved rotations are unchanged.
- **1.13.0**: the Adjust pads on the Race tab: an **Adjust** button (while the race runs or is paused) opens a panel with the lane buttons, the five pads (penalty points, damage points, time, laps, championship points), Undo and the layout buttons, and adjusts the race in the main window directly. The Race Director page and the Race tab use the same panel builder (`renderer/adjustui.js`).

- **1.11.0**: the Leaderboard page is a grid (pick up to 6 columns by 4 rows with little squares, give every cell a row design from the Leaderboard Editor or the classic table, boards that cover more cells, From place and To place per board), the same two settings on the Leaderboard widget, and the race setup folder "Penalties, fuel and pits" is now called "Rule sets"
- **1.10.1**: Home and tabs: the Live Race button has two looks (switched off with no race running, lit while a race is on), the Available cards are gone from Home, the Team choice only shows in a session with teams (an Endurance Race, heats with teams), and the three dropdowns of a lane (Team, Driver, Slotcar) are spaced evenly
- **1.10.0**: the Damage System: damage points from incidents and automatic deslots, levels (light, medium, heavy, critical) that ask for a stop in the pits to repair, a deadline per level, retire or disqualify as the end of a car, a Damage Systems tab, Damage and Repair Timer widgets, audio calls, and the damage in the results
- **1.9.1**: fix for 1.9.0: starting a race showed "This screen could not be opened: An object could not be cloned". The settings of the race that go to the Live Race window held a function (the random generator for "a random driver steps in"), which Electron cannot send. It is left out now, and a snapshot that cannot be kept can never stop a race. The screen tests now check every call of the app interface the way Electron does.
