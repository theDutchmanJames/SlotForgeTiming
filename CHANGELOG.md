# Changelog

Every version of SlotForge Timing, newest first. The README has a section **What to try (x.y.z)** for each version, with what changed, how to try it and what it does not do (yet).

## Versions

- **1.54.0**: **the Push and Save System** (Dutch approved the mock-ups: "It's okay, users can define the amount they want anyway. Yes. No, perfect. Sure"). How hard a driver drives (his lap against his reference lap) changes how fast his tyres and his fuel are used up; lap times and grip are never touched. **Rules and words** (`renderer/pushsave.js`, new UMD `root.PushSave`): `DEFAULTS` (normal pace +3%, most saving +8%, from lap 3, slow limit 150%, yellow saving on, tyres 40/30, fuel 25/20), `PRESETS` standard/gentle/strong, `clean`/`fromConfig` (limits; the most saving pace is always more than normal pace), `paceOf`, `mult` (1 + extra at flat out, 1 at normal pace, 1 - saving from the most saving pace, in a straight line, four decimals), `kind` (PUSH/NORMAL/SAVE with a band of 1%), `lapEffect` (why a lap wears as usual: off, pit, resumed, noref, early, slow; a slow lap under yellow saves) and the words. **Settings** (`schema.js`, `renderer/database.js`, `renderer/editor.js`, `renderer/icons.js`, `renderer/styles.css`): a new table `pushsavesystems` (Race Setup > Push and Save Systems, lime `--c-ps`, after Safety Car Systems; not in the Database tab) with four folders (Basics with the preset buttons, Pace, Wear, Which laps count) and a race setup field `pushSave` (Racing Systems, "No push and save"). **Engine** (`renderer/engine.js`, `renderer/race.js`): `R.pushSave`, `R.psRef` and `R.psInfo`; the reference lap is the record the entry brings (`psRec`: the best lap of the driver on the track from the records, without token or non-personal-best laps, divided by the race speed) or the best lap of the race, whichever is faster, and a lap that beats it is the reference itself (not a lap over the longest lap time); per car `ps` (the pace of every lap, spliced with a removed lap, reset by a restart) and `psNow`; the fuel of a lap is the litres of the Fuel Setup times the pace number times the fuel use of the car (the Fuel Setup's target lap, most extra and most saving are not used); the tyre wear of a lap is multiplied by the pace number; kept in snapshots and sent to phones (`liveshare.js`: the full `cfg` and the car keys `ps`, `psNow`). **Screens:** the lime folder Push and Save on the race screen and the Race Director page (`renderer/pushsaveui.js`, new: the table, the lap by lap list of the last 10 laps), the **Pace meter** widget in the folder Tyres (`renderer/pacewidgets.js`, new `root.PaceW`: badge, gauge, bar, wear only; options and colours), a Leaderboard option **Pace**, the Driver stats cells **Pace** and **Wear now**, the lap review tag, the Home Rules card line, the Help folder, database delete (cascades), export/import (`transfer.js`, `renderer/transferui.js`) and the web server (`READABLE`). **Tests:** `tests/pushsave.test.js`, `tests/pushsavescreen.test.js` and `tests/pacewidgets.test.js` (new); the widgets, delta, rows, driver grid, setup strip, rotation strip, icons, screens and safety car tests and the script lists of 16 screen tests were updated; 161 deliberate breakages (`mut/run22*.py`), all caught except one that changes nothing (a guard of the Pace meter that the engine repeats). **Not verified** in the full Electron window or with real hardware.

- **1.51.1**: **a car held on a Trackmate sensor is a pit stop** (Dutch: on Linux and Windows a car blocking the sensor for the pit hold time just went on counting laps; his two Trackmate logs showed that the box sends the letter again every 256 ms while the sensor is covered, and the app only used the first letter). **Link** (`renderer/trackmate.js`): besides `hit` (the first letter after 500 ms of quiet: the crossing, counted at once) the link now sends `block` (letters keep coming until the hold time has gone by, measured on the clock of the box; time = the first letter, so the stop counts from the arrival, once per stay) and `free` (500 ms of quiet after a block; time = last letter + 128 ms, half the 256 ms letter interval). `L.setHold(ms)` sets the hold time (a number from 0; at least 500 ms; anything else 2000). **Race** (`renderer/race.js`): `hit` is a crossing that may be a stop (`crossCar(i, at, true)` -> `eng.cross(i, t, { holdDue: true })`); `block` -> `holdCar` -> `eng.block(i, t, true)` (only while running, `t` not later than now); `free` -> `unblockCar(i, at)`; lanes through `Trackmate.laneMap`; token sessions ignore both; the hold time is the setup's `pitHold` (`Trackmate.link.setHold(pitMs)` when the race starts with Trackmate). **Engine** (`renderer/engine.js`): `R.block(i, t, crossed)` starts the stop without counting the crossing again and drops the tyre check that waited; `R.cross(..., { holdDue })` keeps the tyre-due penalty of a crossing on the due lap back (`c.wxHold`) until it is known whether the car stays; `wxFlush` gives it as of the crossing when `max(pitHold, 500) + 700` ms have gone by without a stop (in `R.tick`), at the next crossing and at the end of the race. The Trackmate page text mentions it. **Logs:** the two logs are in docs/trackmate-logs/ (with a README) and are replayed in tests/trackmate.test.js (the monitor leaves out the steady 200 ms ticks, so the replay puts them back). Tests: tests/trackmate.test.js (link, both logs, wiring, text), tests/weather.test.js (held tyre penalty, block with crossed), tests/live.test.js (block and free through the race screen).
